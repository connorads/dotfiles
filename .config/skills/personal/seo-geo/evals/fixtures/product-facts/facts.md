# Rotaly product facts (approved by product, Sept 2026)

Use only these facts for marketing copy. Anything not here needs checking with product before it ships.

## What Rotaly is

Rota and shift scheduling software for hospitality and retail teams in the UK and Ireland. Web app for managers, iOS and Android app for staff.

## Pricing (per active staff member per month, billed monthly, ex VAT)

| Plan | Price | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | £2.50 | Rota builder, staff app, shift swaps, holiday requests |
| Pro | £4.00 | Starter + clock in/out (tablet or app, with GPS geofence), timesheets, payroll export |
| Multi-site | £5.50 | Pro + cross-site cover, labour cost vs sales reports, area manager view |

- 14-day free trial, no card needed.
- Annual billing: 2 months free.
- Minimum 5 billable staff per site.

## Integrations (live)

- Payroll export: Xero, Sage 50, BrightPay (CSV for others).
- POS sales import for labour cost: Square, Lightspeed Restaurant.
- Calendar feed (iCal) for staff.

## Not supported (be honest about these)

- No payroll processing - Rotaly exports hours, it does not run payroll.
- No US, Canadian or Australian award/overtime rules. UK and Ireland working time rules only.
- No HR document storage or onboarding e-signatures.
- No offline mode in the manager web app.

## Numbers we can use

- 1,400 sites use Rotaly (internal billing data, 1 Sept 2026).
- Median time to build a weekly rota from a template: 11 minutes (product analytics, July 2026, 312 sites).

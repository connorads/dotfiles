## Stable failures

### Artefact shape

Claude's current-skill ticket added a preamble, title, mandatory status line,
new evidence subsections, two mechanism sections, checklists, notes, and a
closing offer despite the supplied three-heading shape. It said: "the skill
requires the status line". Codex's current-skill PR omitted all three supplied
headings. The body is steering agents toward the skill's preferred ticket shape
instead of preserving the caller's artefact.

### Authority is absent from the model

Every Claude arm and every Codex arm invented at least one decision or
commitment in the meeting-summary case. Representative current-skill lines:

- Claude: "Maya raised it as an option and I'm going with it."
- Claude: "We didn't assign an owner, so I'm taking it."
- Codex: "I'll own the migration docs."

The prompt established no authority for the writer to make those decisions.
The current voice rule protects unexpressed beliefs and checks but does not
protect decisions, assignments, promises, deadlines, or claims of consensus.

### Calibration mostly works without the skill

Both clients' bare arms usually kept incident causality, vendor attribution,
verification scope, and decisive evidence calibrated. The current skill still
adds value when evidence and a proposed fix contradict each other, but much of
its existing explanation restates behaviour the current models already supply.

### The ad-hoc sentence is insufficient

The ad-hoc arm did not solve authority. Claude volunteered the writer for the
documentation and assigned Lee; Codex wrote "I'm happy to take on the migration
docs." It also failed to preserve the PR template in Codex. A compact skill can
still earn its place if it fixes these failures without reintroducing the
current structure and verbosity.

## Revision target

The revision needs one model that transfers across artefacts:

1. Classify each consequential statement by epistemic basis - observed,
   attributed, inferred, or unverified.
2. Check separately whether the named speaker has authority for any action,
   decision, agreement, assignment, deadline, or commitment.
3. Preserve the supplied artefact and express those distinctions in its
   natural prose.
4. Spend words in proportion to the consequence of a mistake.

The held-out ADR, handoff, and casual-message prompts remain unread during the
revision pass.

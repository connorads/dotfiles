# Evidence and reporting

## Retain what another tester needs

For each run, keep build identity, start-state provenance, controls, information
available to the player agent, timing regime and relevant environment. Record
requested settings such as mute. Save ordered actions with durations and the
images or clips actually reviewed. Add logs or state only where they explain a
finding. Retain the first visible divergence, not just a final error screen.

A reproduction should identify:

- The ordinary route or prepared starting setup.
- The input sequence and any timing conditions.
- Expected behaviour and its basis.
- Observed behaviour, evidence location and remaining alternative explanations.
- The repair and replay result, when a repair was authorised.

Group repeated symptoms with the same demonstrated cause. Rank findings by
player consequence, such as blocked progression or unreliable required input,
not by how striking a screenshot looks.

## State results precisely

Use concrete statements: "Reached the seal from a fresh start using keyboard
movement; camera navigation used read-only position data. The next route was
not attempted." Or: "A prepared encounter completed at normal speed. Equipment
acquisition and the path to the arena remain untested."

Separate confirmed defects, suspected issues, controller/environment limitations
and untested content. Explain any missing evidence needed to resolve a concern.
Do not infer enjoyment, onboarding comprehension or human win rates from an
agent completing the game. Reading a hint successfully does not establish that
players notice or understand it.

Keep the playable build, launch instructions and controls. An evidence index
should make the important before/after observations easy to review without
reading every tool log. Describe an outstanding blocker specifically; avoid
converting an incomplete run into a reassuring overall pass.

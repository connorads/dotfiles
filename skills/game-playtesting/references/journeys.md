# Journeys and investigation

## Cover transitions

Keep an ordered ledger with the reached milestone, required next action,
observed result and evidence. Include the connecting traversal, not just visits
to destinations. If a test starts at a prepared boss arena, its result concerns
that arena; the route, equipment and prerequisites remain separate questions.

A completed route is one witnessed route under recorded conditions. It does
not establish all routes or player strategies. Use a fresh start for integrated
qualification. Check saved continuation separately from uninterrupted play.

Choose focused checks from the live game's risks:

- Control hand-offs: hold movement, pause, release, resume; open a menu during
  action; lose focus; recover from defeat.
- Geometry: required steps, narrow passages, ledges, wall contact and movement
  away from contact. Observe the rendered body as well as collision state.
- Camera: orbit near obstructions, look while moving, and return from overlays.
- Progression: activate a prerequisite, traverse its newly available route and
  use the next interaction. Retry or continue after a failure where supported.

This is a selection guide, not a requirement to exercise irrelevant controls.
Quietness, deliberate animation recovery and a difficult encounter are not
failures merely because progress takes time.

## Investigate a specific contradiction

Example: movement stops beside a wall after the player turns away.

Record the approach and input release, whether the avatar visibly turns, whether
movement was accepted, and whether collision still reports contact. Repeat from
a nearby ordinary position. A missed key-up, an active attack recovery and a
collision error predict different results. Select the cheapest observation that
separates them before changing movement speed.

For an apparently missed attack, align input receipt, active hit time, facing,
range and displayed contact. Equal damage numbers do not make changed geometry
a presentation-only repair. For a sluggish action, distinguish initial
acknowledgement from acceleration, active time and recovery.

An unusual picture is a candidate observation. State the rule it might violate
and inspect the surrounding motion. Stylised impacts and realm transformations
may be intentional. Use the player's instructions and established rules as the
expectation, rather than treating every existing implementation detail as correct.

## Replay the repair

Retain the failing case before editing. Verify the relevant behaviour through
its stable interface, then replay the visible interaction and the affected next
milestone. A regression test can isolate the defect; the surrounding journey
checks that the repair still composes with the game.

If the controller cannot execute the action, report that limitation and use a
labelled diagnostic method. Do not remove collision, enemies, gates or cooldowns
just to make the automated route pass.

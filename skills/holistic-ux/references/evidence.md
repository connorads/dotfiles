# Evidence for retained guidance

Read this only when using or reviewing a factual framework claim. It maps each
retained rule to its source and scope. The operational rules in this skill are
deliberately narrower than the surrounding UX literature.

## Evidence status and synthesis

- GOV.UK separates observations, findings and actions when analysing user
  research. Its procedure requires notes to record what was seen or heard rather
  than an interpretation. Source: [Analyse a research
  session](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/analyse-a-research-session).
- Research methods answer different questions. Behavioural versus attitudinal,
  qualitative versus quantitative, and context of use are separate dimensions.
  Source: [Nielsen Norman Group, When to Use Which User-Experience Research
  Methods](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/which-ux-research-methods/).
- Repeatable usability benchmarks combine task success, time, abandonment,
  false success and participant ratings, then compare later rounds consistently.
  Source: [GOV.UK, Usability benchmarking a website or whole
  service](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/measuring-success/usability-benchmarking-a-website-or-whole-service).

Scope: these sources support separating evidence from interpretation and
matching measures to questions. They do not create a universal hierarchy in
which one method always outranks another.

## Service mapping

- Service blueprinting represents customer-visible evidence, customer actions,
  frontstage contact, backstage activity and support processes separated by
  interaction and visibility lines. Source: [Lynn Shostack, Designing Services
  That Deliver](https://hbr.org/1984/01/designing-services-that-deliver).
- Whole-service mapping includes online and offline touchpoints, backend
  processes, involved people and evidence users must provide. Source: [GOV.UK,
  Map and understand a user's whole
  problem](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/design/map-a-users-whole-problem).
- Experience maps combine evidence from several users and are most useful for
  extended, multi-part services. Source: [GOV.UK, Creating an experience
  map](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/creating-an-experience-map).

Scope: lane definitions shape an artefact. They do not license inventing the
contents of a lane.

## Inclusive and ethical research

- User needs remain based on user research rather than stakeholder assumptions,
  and research includes people who struggle to use existing services. Source:
  [GOV.UK, Learning about users and their
  needs](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/start-by-learning-user-needs).
- Research notes, recordings and participant details are personal data. Teams
  define consent, access, storage, anonymisation, retention and deletion.
  Source: [GOV.UK, Managing user research data and participant
  privacy](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/managing-user-research-data-participant-privacy).
- Sensitive research requires explicit preparation for distress, safeguarding
  and stopping a session. Source: [GOV.UK, Researching emotionally sensitive
  subjects](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/researching-emotionally-sensitive-subjects).

## Claims deliberately removed

The skill does not teach a catalogue of UX laws. The audit found that the old
reference turned scoped findings into general prescriptions:

- A meta-analysis found peak-end effects but did not support replacing the
  overall average as the universal account of retrospective evaluation. Source:
  [Alaybek et al., 2022](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0749597822000334).
- Norman distinguishes affordances, which are actor-object action possibilities,
  from signifiers that communicate where action occurs. Source: [Don Norman,
  Affordances and Design](https://jnd.org/affordances-and-design/).
- Contemporary cognitive-load accounts do not retain germane load as a third
  additive load type. The theory concerns learning and instruction, so interface
  prescriptions require an explicit translation. Source: [Skulmowski and Xu,
  2022](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09624-7).
- Nielsen's 0.1, 1 and 10 second response-time limits are not the Doherty
  Threshold. Source: [Nielsen Norman Group, Response Times: The 3 Important
  Limits](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/response-times-3-important-limits/).
- Meadows presents leverage points as a caveated invitation to think, not a
  five-item universal UX ranking. Source: [Donella Meadows, Leverage Points:
  Places to Intervene in a
  System](https://donellameadows.org/archives/leverage-points-places-to-intervene-in-a-system/).
- Cynefin domains describe constraints and causal conditions in context, not
  fixed categories assigned from task names. Source: [The Cynefin Co, Cynefin
  Domains](https://cynefin.io/wiki/Cynefin_Domains).

Reintroduce a removed claim only after a baseline failure shows the skill needs
it, the claim is scoped to the source, and an ablation shows the instruction
changes behaviour.
